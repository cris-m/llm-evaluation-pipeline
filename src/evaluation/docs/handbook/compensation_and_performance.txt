Acme Robotics Employee Handbook: Compensation and Performance

Performance Reviews:
Performance reviews happen twice per year, in June and December. The December review determines salary adjustments and bonus eligibility. Employees write a self-assessment two weeks before each review meeting. Ratings use a 5-point scale, where 3 means "meets expectations."

Annual Bonus:
Full-time employees are eligible for an annual bonus targeted at 8% of base salary for individual contributors and 15% for managers. Bonuses are paid in the first paycheck of March. Employees must be employed on December 31 of the performance year to receive the bonus. Employees rated 1 or 2 in December are not eligible.

Retirement Plan:
The company offers a 401(k) plan. It matches 100% of employee contributions up to 4% of salary. Matching contributions vest after 2 years of service. Employees are eligible to enroll on the first day of the month after their hire date.

Promotions:
Promotion decisions are made once per year, effective April 1. An employee must have spent at least 12 months in their current level and have received a rating of 4 or higher in the most recent December review.

Pay Schedule:
Employees are paid twice per month, on the 15th and the last business day of the month.
