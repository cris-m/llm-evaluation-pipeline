Acme Robotics Employee Handbook: General Policies

Time Off Policy:
New employees receive 15 vacation days per year. After 3 years of continuous service, this increases to 20 days per year. Up to 5 unused vacation days may be carried over into the next calendar year; any days above 5 are forfeited on December 31. The company is officially closed between December 25 and January 1. These closure days do not count against an employee's accrued vacation balance.

Sick Leave:
Every employee receives 10 paid sick days per year, separate from vacation. Sick days do not carry over. A doctor's note is required only if an employee is out for more than 3 consecutive days.

Public Holidays:
The company observes 9 paid public holidays: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, the day after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day.

Remote Work Policy:
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. However, Tuesdays and Thursdays are mandatory in-office days for all staff, regardless of role. The primary headquarters is located in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and a second regional office is located in Austin, Texas. Working remotely from outside the United States for more than 10 days per year requires written approval from HR for tax and legal reasons.

Parental Leave:
The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid leave. The secondary caregiver receives 8 weeks of fully paid leave. This leave can begin up to two weeks before the expected birth or adoption date. Leave must be used within 12 months of the birth or adoption date.

Benefits Enrollment:
Health insurance coverage starts on the first day of the month immediately following your official hire date. For example, an employee hired on March 18 is covered starting April 1. Dental and vision coverage are automatically included in this package at no extra cost to the employee. Employees have 30 days from their hire date to add dependents.
