Acme Robotics Employee Handbook: Offices and Facilities

Pittsburgh Headquarters:
The Pittsburgh headquarters houses engineering, the machining area, and the main robot test lab. Badge access to the building is available from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM on weekdays. Employee parking is free in the lot behind the building; visitor parking is in the front lot.

Austin Regional Office:
The Austin office is home to the sales and customer support teams and a small demo lab with two robot cells. The office is open from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM on weekdays. Parking in Austin is validated for up to $12 per day.

Visitors:
All visitors must sign in at reception, wear a visitor badge, and be escorted by an employee. Visitors may not photograph the lab or any product designs.

Meeting Rooms:
Meeting rooms are booked through the company calendar. Rooms are automatically released if no one checks in within 10 minutes of the start time.
